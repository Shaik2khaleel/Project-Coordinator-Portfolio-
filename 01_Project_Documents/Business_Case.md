# Business Case

## Project Name
Digital Transformation & E-Commerce Website Implementation

## Business Problem
The company relied only on physical stores, limiting customer reach and online sales.

## Proposed Solution
Develop a secure e-commerce website with online ordering, payment integration, and customer account management.

## Business Benefits
- Increase online sales
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Expand market reach
- Reduce manual work
- Improve business efficiency

## Estimated Budget
AED 500,000

## Estimated Duration
6 Months

## Success Criteria
- Website launched successfully
- Online payments working
- Customer registration enabled
- 95% system uptime
- Positive customer feedback

## Recommendation
Proceed with the project because it will improve business growth and customer experience.
